# CanvasDraft

Use this compact structure for each direction. Match the user's language.

```text
Name / scene metaphor:
Composition: [small wireframe: content zone, focal subject, depth layers]
Palette and material:
Idle motion: [what moves, rhythm, amplitude]
Pointer behavior: [local influence, response, return on leave]
Mobile / reduced motion:
Renderer and reason:
Strength / cost:
```

## Direction families

These are starting points, not templates to repeat for every request.

- **Living landscape:** recognizable sky, terrain, clouds, water, plants, or celestial body. Establish foreground, middle distance, and background with coherent light. Move layers at different rates; use bounded pointer parallax or local wind. Keep a quiet region behind the title.
- **Engraved field:** fine contour lines, ripples, or topographic strokes whose local curvature responds to the pointer. Use a deliberate density gradient and a clear focal structure rather than filling every pixel equally.
- **Material study:** ink, silk, mist, refracted light, or grain with physically coherent behavior. Define the material and force first. Use shaders only when their pixel-level work earns the cost.
- **Constructed geometry:** modular planes, orbiting forms, or a controlled particle structure. Give particles a composition and purpose; avoid arbitrary dots connected to every neighbor.

## Reference interpretation

The founding user reference was [Anthropic's Fable/Mythos launch page](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1). On 2026-09-16, the observed hero showed a blue sky, illustrated clouds, a moon, large centered editorial typography, and time-of-day controls. The user selected **ambient background animation and mouse interaction** as the desired scope, not the rest of the site or its content.

Treat this as an example of a coherent living scene, not a mandated style or evidence of a specific rendering technology. Revisit the live reference if a new task asks for fidelity. Do not require time-of-day controls, a moon, or a blue palette in unrelated work.

## Composition checks

- Can the scene be recognized in a still frame?
- Does its motion reinforce its subject rather than distract from reading?
- Is the pointer response visible but bounded, with no jumps on entry or leave?
- Does the narrow crop retain the intended focal point and content space?
- Is the static/reduced-motion version a finished design rather than an empty rectangle?
