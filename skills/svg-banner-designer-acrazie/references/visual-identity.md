## Design Principles for Banners

1. **Orthogonal & Modular Geometry**:
   - Heavy, wide typography with structured geometric builds.
   - Clean rectangular containers, chamfered corner cuts, or tech-inspired border accents.

2. **Offset Echo Layers**:
   - Multi-layered vector depth: Primary elements (titles, badge borders, or focal shapes) cast 2 to 3 discrete offset echo silhouettes (shifted southeast or south) in alternating shades of the palette.
   - Creates a tactile, screen-printed or retro-futuristic depth without relying on generic drop-shadow blurs.

3. **Sunset Gradients & Ambient Glows**:
   - Gradient vectors transitioning vertically or diagonally from warm amber/saffron at the top or focal point, flowing into rich royal violet and dark amethyst bases.
   - Subtle radial background glow centered behind the focal text or illustration to separate foreground from deep canvas darkness.

4. **Tech-Grid Backgrounds**:
   - Crisp, low-opacity SVG `<pattern>` grids (e.g. 24px or 32px pitch in `#7b2cbf` at `10-15%` opacity) providing scale and architectural precision.
   - Accents such as coordinate ticks, crosshairs (`+`), or subtle telemetry markers.
